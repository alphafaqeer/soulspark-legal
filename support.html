Manage or cancel a subscription

  • iPhone/iPad: Open system Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SoulSpark → Cancel Subscription. Open Apple subscriptions.
  • Android: Open Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → SoulSpark → Cancel subscription. Open Google Play subscriptions.

The app's Manage subscription button also opens store management. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Deleting SoulSpark or clearing its data does not cancel a subscription. Actual billing uses the localized price shown by your store; the monthly reference US price is $0.99. Apple and Google handle refund requests through their support channels.

Restore Premium

  1. Check that the original purchasing Apple or Google account is signed in on the device.
  2. Connect to the internet and open SoulSpark.
  3. Go to Settings → Restore purchases, or choose Restore purchases on the Premium screen.
  4. Allow the store check to finish. Use Refresh subscription status or refresh store availability if needed.
  5. If Premium remains unavailable, check whether your subscription is active, pending, expired or affected by a payment issue. Contact us before purchasing again.

Restoring does not charge you again. Purchases do not automatically transfer between Apple and Google stores. Legacy Android subscribers should use their original Google account; unresolved ownership or verification issues require support. Android server verification may be unavailable in builds where it has not been configured.

Reminders are not arriving

Enable reminders and choose times in SoulSpark Settings. Allow notifications in system Settings; the app provides an Open notification settings button. Check Focus/Do Not Disturb, notification summaries, Android battery restrictions and volume settings. The operating system controls delivery timing, so exact delivery cannot be guaranteed. Free access supports one reminder; Premium supports up to five.

Spoken sessions and saved content

For spoken sessions, check Premium status, media volume, silent-mode behavior and installed device voices. Sessions run in the foreground and can pause on interruption. The bundled affirmation library works offline; stores and other online services still need a connection.

Favorites, streaks and preferences live on the device and do not have app-managed cloud sync. Try Retry local storage if the app reports a storage error. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app until you understand that local records can be lost.

Privacy choices and deletion

Use Settings → Advertising privacy choices to open Google's privacy form where required. Review iOS Tracking or Android advertising controls in system Settings. Premium removes in-app ads while its entitlement is verified.

Use Clear saved affirmations to erase favorites. For all local app records, clear app storage on Android or delete the app on iOS, considering any operating-system backups. This does not cancel subscriptions or delete records held by Apple, Google or other providers. Email us for personal-data requests; see the Privacy Policy for details.
