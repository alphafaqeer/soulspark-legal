Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Your daily reflection is personal. This policy explains how SoulSpark 2.0 for iOS and Android handles information, including information processed by advertising and app-store services.

SoulSpark's developer operates the app and can be contacted at soulsparkdev01@gmail.com. This policy also covers this legal and support website. Older app versions may behave differently.

1. Your on-device library

Affirmations are bundled with the app and can be read offline. Favorites, selected topics, reminder schedules, theme and background choices, session settings, onboarding status and daily streak dates/counts are saved locally using device storage. These records are not uploaded to a SoulSpark content database or cloud-sync service by the current app. Local app storage is not a secure vault; device backups and device security settings may affect it.

The current app has no account-registration or sign-in screen, and does not integrate Firebase Analytics or a separate general-purpose analytics SDK. This does not mean no data is collected: advertising, purchases, hosting and support have their own data processing described below.

2. Advertising and device information

Free users may see Google AdMob banners on Today, Explore and Saved after applicable consent checks. Verified Premium subscribers see no ads. Depending on permissions, consent, region and SDK behavior, Google Mobile Ads may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, diagnostics, crash and performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data collection; refusing tracking does not make all advertising anonymous.

Google processes information under its own policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. We do not add our own sale of your saved affirmations or preferences. Advertising disclosures or rights involving sale, sharing or targeted advertising depend on applicable law and Google's actual processing.

3. Consent and privacy choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check applicable consent requirements before requesting ads. Where required, use Settings → Advertising privacy choices to review or change your choices. The form may not be required in every region. Ad availability may be limited when consent cannot be obtained.

On iOS, tracking across other companies' apps or websites requires your App Tracking Transparency permission where applicable. SoulSpark checks existing permission before requesting it; the current release workflow disables the app's optional tracking request. You can review permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android offers advertising privacy controls in system Settings. Revoking permission does not automatically delete data previously held by a provider.

4. Purchases and verification

Apple App Store or Google Play processes subscriptions and payment details. SoulSpark receives product, transaction, renewal, expiry and entitlement information to unlock Premium and restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card details. iOS uses Apple StoreKit through expo-iap; no paid third-party subscription service is used.

Android secure-verification integration code is present but is not operational in the current unconfigured 2.0 app. In a build where it is enabled with authenticated identity, verification sends an authentication token, purchase token and product identifier over HTTPS. The backend uses Google Play's Developer API and Firebase Authentication/Firestore ownership records to prevent purchases unlocking unrelated accounts; these records can contain an account ID, hashed purchase-token reference, product and creation time. This is purchase verification, not Firebase Analytics. It is not required by the iOS purchase path. We will update this policy if materially different services are enabled.

Apple and Google retain store records under their own policies: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Reminders, speech and sharing

Reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device after notification permission. The current app does not register remote push tokens or operate a remote push service. You can disable reminders in the app or revoke notifications in system Settings. Delivery timing is controlled by the operating system.

Spoken sessions use the device's text-to-speech service; SoulSpark does not record your microphone or send recordings to its own server. Downloaded or network-based voices may follow your operating system's settings and provider terms. Sharing creates a temporary affirmation image and opens your system share sheet. The destination app receives what you choose to share under its own policy. Copying text places it on the device clipboard, which your operating system or other apps may access.

6. Support and this website

If you email support, we process your address, message and any diagnostic details or attachments you voluntarily send to respond and resolve your request. Email is handled through our Gmail mailbox. Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers or purchase tokens.

This static website uses GitHub Pages. It has no forms, ad scripts, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or cookies added by SoulSpark. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical request logs for hosting and security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to other sites are governed by their policies.

7. Retention, deletion and security

Local preferences remain until cleared, app data is removed or the app is uninstalled, subject to operating-system backup/restore behavior. Settings includes Clear saved affirmations for favorites; removing all local records requires clearing app storage on Android or deleting the app on iOS. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription or erase store/provider records.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and reasonable follow-up, legal or security needs, then deleted or de-identified. If backend ownership records are enabled, retention is limited to purchase integrity, disputes and legal obligations; deletion may require retaining restricted records to prevent fraud. Third-party providers set their own retention periods. No universal deletion period is promised for their records.

We use appropriate safeguards, including encrypted transport for online requests, but no system is completely secure. Avoid putting sensitive information in support messages or shared content.

8. Your rights and international processing

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain targeted-advertising/sale/sharing activities. Contact soulsparkdev01@gmail.com; we may need proportionate identity verification. Local-only records are normally accessible only on your device, so we cannot remotely retrieve or erase them. Contact Apple, Google or GitHub for information they control independently. You may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Where GDPR or similar law applies, processing relies on consent for permission-dependent advertising/tracking, contractual necessity for requested purchases/support, and legitimate interests for proportionate security and fraud prevention, subject to applicable law. Providers may process data internationally, including in the United States, using their applicable transfer safeguards. We do not promise that all data stays in your country.

9. Children's privacy

SoulSpark is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 or a higher minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. This statement is not an age-verification or parental-consent system.

10. Policy changes and contact

We will post changes here with a revised effective date and provide additional notice where required. For privacy questions or requests, email soulsparkdev01@gmail.com.
